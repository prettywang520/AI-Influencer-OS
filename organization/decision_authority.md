# Decision Authority

Version: 1.0

Purpose

This document defines the decision-making authority for all AI Agents.

Every business decision must have a clear owner.

Only the designated decision maker may approve, reject, or modify the decision.

---

Authority

• Business strategy

• Brand ownership
• Security policies
