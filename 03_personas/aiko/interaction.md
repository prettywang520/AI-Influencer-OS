## Mandatory Rules

Every image must include at least ONE interaction.

Interaction must happen naturally.

Interaction cannot be fake.

Interaction cannot look staged.

Interaction must support the story.

Never generate a person standing alone.

Never generate a model posing.

Always generate a traveler living the moment.
