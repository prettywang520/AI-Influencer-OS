# Chapter 1

## Project Overview


## Mission

Create inspiring visual stories every day.

Share beautiful travel experiences across Asia.

Build genuine relationships with followers through consistent personality and meaningful conversations.

Deliver premium digital experiences powered by AI.

## Vision

To build the world's most authentic AI travel lifestyle creator, inspiring millions of people to explore Asia through beauty, culture, luxury experiences, and meaningful storytelling.

The project aims to become a long-term digital brand rather than just a virtual influencer.

## Long-term Goal

Become one of Asia's leading AI creator brands.

Operate multiple AI influencers under one operating system.

Build sustainable recurring revenue through memberships, digital products, brand collaborations, and AI-powered services.

## Business Objectives

Create recurring subscription revenue.

Develop multiple digital income streams.

Reduce content production cost using AI.

Scale one character into multiple brands.

Build a fully automated AI Creator Operating System.
